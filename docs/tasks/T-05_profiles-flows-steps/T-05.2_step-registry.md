## Goal

Replace the tuples of `phases/catalog.py` with a registry the step files fill themselves. **No behaviour change** (PR-005 section 11, S1).

## Files

| Action | Path |
|---|---|
| Create | `application/orchestration/phases/registry.py` (`register_step`, `STEPS`, `step(name)`, `steps_of_kind(kind)`) |
| Modify | every file of `application/orchestration/phases/**` that has the constants at the top: it registers itself with its constants (`name`, `kind`, `legacy_id`, `prompt`, `schemas`, `required`, `model_env_var`, `default_model`) |
| Modify | `application/orchestration/phases/catalog.py` (reads the registry; `PHASES`, `PHASE_NAMES`, `ALL_PHASE_NAMES`, `CONVERSATION_PHASES` keep their shape) |
| Modify | `tests/test_phase_structure.py` (tests the registry instead of the tuples) |

## Specification

- A step registers once; a second registration of a name is an error at import. The registry is filled by importing the step packages; nothing else lists steps.
- The registry knows the three kinds of PR-005 section 3.2: `llm`, `llm_per_action`, `code`. The motion validator and the gesture builder are `code`; the worker, the MCP operator and the data engineer are `llm_per_action`; the answer checker and the clarification stay as they are (they become hooks in T-05.12).
- `legacy_id` is the old `PHASE_ID`, kept as a label (Q07-9).
- `DEFAULT_MODEL` stays where it is until T-05.5 removes it.

## Acceptance criteria

The suite is green; `tests/golden/flow_golden.json` is **byte-identical**; `git grep -n "TEXT_FLOW_MODULES"` finds nothing.

The baseline of the [tasks README](../README.md) section 3, plus a test that registering a name twice fails.
