## Goal

Replace the tuples of `phases/catalog.py` with a registry the step files fill themselves. **No behaviour change** (PR-005 section 11, S1).

| Action | Path |

## Specification

- A step registers once; a second registration of a name is an error at import. The registry is filled by importing the step packages; nothing else lists steps.
- The registry knows the three kinds of PR-005 section 3.2: `llm`, `llm_per_action`, `code`. The motion validator and the gesture builder are `code`; the worker, the MCP operator and the data engineer are `llm_per_action`; the answer checker and the clarification stay as they are (they become hooks in T-05.12).
- `legacy_id` is the old `PHASE_ID`, kept as a label (Q07-9).
- `DEFAULT_MODEL` stays where it is until T-05.5 removes it.
