## Goal

Reach 20 s or less on the real models without losing the quality of the answer. **Ask the maintainer first: every run costs money (about 0.05 USD a message).**

## What to do

1. Baseline: the simple message ("How are my orders? I'm CL-1001") 5 times on `dynamics` and 5 times on `dynamics_fast` (the local Dynamics service, set in the shell: the `.env` URL points at an older service). Write the seconds per step (T-08.2) and the median in *Notes*.
2. If the median of `dynamics_fast` is above 20 s, change **one thing** and run again, both reports kept: the size of the selector's input (the tools it is shown, the length of their descriptions), the length of the prompts, the model of one step. A change that is worse is reverted, not tuned in place.
3. Read the answers against the points of the fixed messages (the maintainer ticks them): the right tool, the language, short and speakable, `Pendiente` told as the carrier has not answered.
4. Stop when the median is 20 s or less and the answers are right, or when three changes did not move it: then say so and decide with the maintainer (the alternatives of the ADR: a single tool-using step, or the triage and the gate).

## Acceptance criteria

The median of 5 runs of the simple message on `dynamics_fast` is 20 s or less, or the *Notes* say why not and what was decided; every change has its before and after.

## Verification

The reports in `tests/output/` (git-ignored) and the seconds in *Notes*.
