## Specification

- `google_free`: `default` = `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (triage, data engineer, answer checker, clarification, emotion reader); `gemini-3.8-flash` for `project_manager`, `safety_quality_gatekeeper`, `cognitive_worker`, `mcp_operator`, `draft_writer`, `editor_in_chief` and `motion_planner`.
- Its `_about` says what it needs (`GOOGLE_API_KEY`, `GOOGLE_URL`), that it is untested as a combination, the free-tier terms (content used to improve Google's products; no published rate limits) and that the price of `gemini-3.8-flash` doubles on 2027-01-01.
- `budget50_groq` and the other profiles stay as they are (T-02.6 reviews them).

## Acceptance criteria

Every step resolves to a Google id that is in the catalog; the profile has no unknown key; `effective_models()` names `google_free` as the source for every step; the two documents name it as active.

The baseline of the [tasks README](../README.md) section 3, plus `windows\Scripts\python.exe tests\manual\show_models.py` (no key needed).
