## Goal

Take the six decisions that change what is built, and record them in *Resolution*.

> The questions and the facts behind them are in [PR-004](../../proposals/PR-004-model-catalog-gemini-free-tier.md), sections 3 and 8.

## Questions

1. **Which Gemini models are the default.** A billed key (paid tier), or models with a free tier, or only the models without a free tier?
2. **Mistral, OpenAI and Ollama ids.** Remove until verified, or keep and mark unverified?
3. **Which profile is active.** Keep `budget50_groq` or add a Google-only profile?
4. **Dated prices.** Build a price schedule for 2026-12-01 and 2027-01-01 in `prices.py` and the generators, or document it as a known gap?
5. **The free testing provider** now that GitHub Models is gone.
6. **Priced models that are not in the catalog.** Add them (a DeepSeek provider and verified ids), or leave them out of use?

## Resolution

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-27.

1. **Free tier, Google.** After seeing the paid prices and the free-tier terms, the default is the Google models on the free tier: `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` for triage, data engineer, answer checker, clarification and emotion reader; `gemini-3.8-flash` for project manager, safety gate, cognitive worker, MCP operator, draft writer, editor in chief and motion planner. `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` and `gemini-4-argon` stay in the catalog and the price file, not as defaults.
2. **Search them; where information is missing or partial, delete.** Searched: OpenAI complete (kept as listed in PR-004 3.2), Ollama names exist (kept), Mistral partial (**deleted**: no confirmed API id and no price).
3. **A Google-only profile, made active** (`google_free`). `budget50_groq` stays defined, not active. Built in T-02.2.
4. **Not for now.** A known gap in `requirements.md`; `prices.py` is not changed.
5. **The Google ones.**
6. **Without all the information a model is not used: comment it.** Python: a comment with the reason. JSON price files cannot hold comments, so the entry moves to a `_disabled` list that the loaders ignore.

## Acceptance criteria

The six answers are written in *Resolution* with the date; the later tasks follow them.
