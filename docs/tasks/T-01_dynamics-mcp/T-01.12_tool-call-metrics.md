## Goal

A slow or failing tool is visible next to the cost, in the logs, in `SessionMetrics` and in Langfuse, without any value. Decided in Q03-9 B.

## Specification

- Per call: the **tool name**, the **outcome kind** (`ok`, `timeout`, `input`, `not_found`, `retry_later`, `source_problem`, `internal`, `transport`) and the **duration**. **Never** the arguments or the answer (Q02-7).
- The outcome kind of a tool error is read from the text `error (<kind>): ...` that the Go service sends; anything else is `internal`.
- A log line per call (the tool, the outcome, the duration).
- `SessionMetrics` keeps the list of tool calls of the message next to the tokens.
- Langfuse (when its keys are set): a span per tool call named after the tool, with the outcome and the duration as metadata and no input or output. A failure of the reporter never breaks a call.

## Acceptance criteria

A scripted call records one entry with the right kind and a duration; no log line, metric or span holds an argument or an answer; the reporter failing does not break the call.
